Provider Load Balancing Demo

See why a new provider, or a change in weekly hours, makes every new patient go to one provider.

About this demo: the problem and the fix

How distribution works

Every new patient goes to the provider with the lowest load factor (patients ÷ weekly hours). A provider with more hours naturally receives more patients.

The problem

A new provider starts with 0 patients, so their load factor is 0. Until it catches up with everyone else, every new patient goes only to them. The same happens when an existing provider's hours increase: their load factor suddenly drops. The result is a burst to one provider instead of an even flow.

What this demo shows

A history of events (before → after), how load factors change after a provider is added or hours are edited, and where the next patients go. Click any history entry to inspect the state at that moment.

Possible fixes

Fair start: a new provider starts with a realistic patient count (hours × average load factor of the others).
Batch cap: no provider gets more than K patients in a row.
Smooth weighted round-robin: each batch is spread in proportion to hours.
